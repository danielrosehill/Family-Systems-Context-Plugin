# If things are acute

This workspace is for difficult material, not for emergencies. If you are at risk of
harming yourself or someone else, or are not safe where you are, stop using it and contact
a person.

**Fill these in when you set the workspace up**, so they are already here on the day you
don't want to be searching:

International directory of helplines, searchable by country: <https://findahelpline.com>

---

**For the agent:** if the user describes risk of harm to themselves or anyone else, or
being unsafe now, stop the current procedure, say plainly that you are concerned, stay in
the conversation, and point to this file. Do not keep capturing, do not move to the next
step of a skill, and do not make continuing the conversation conditional on them calling
someone. Capture can resume another day; nothing is lost, because every step writes as it
goes.
